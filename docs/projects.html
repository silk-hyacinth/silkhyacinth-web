A catalog of examples of line bundle extension behavior under the Satake-Baily-Borel compactification of many period map constructions

project for Math+ 2026 with Aryan Agarwal, William Anderson, George Hall, Michael Klausner, Lauren Schwartz, Austin Setzler, with amazing guidance by Prof. Collen Robles, Prof. Farid HosseiniJafari, and Jin Lee (my goat). we take manifolds and lattices constructed in special ways to have wanted Hodge information, compactify the manifolds, and then look at what happens to the natural Hodge line bundles during this process. i looked at the constructions with Q[\sqrt{d}, i] fields and the quaternion algebras.
